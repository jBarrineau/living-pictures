How many pieces?

Drag a piece onto the board — or tap a piece, then tap where it should go.

How to play

  • Slide a piece: press on it and move it to its place on the board. Let go, and if it's in the right spot it settles in and wakes up.
  • Or just tap: tap a piece once to pick it up (it lifts and glows), then tap the spot on the board where it belongs.
  • Changed your mind? While holding a piece, tap a different piece to pick that one instead.
